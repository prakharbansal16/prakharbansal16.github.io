About Me

Hey there! I'm Prakhar, a third-year Physics Ph.D. candidate at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor, where I work with my supervisor, Dragan Huterer, in the Michigan Cosmology Group. I grew up in Bharatpur, a small town in the eastern part of Rajasthan, India. After completing my Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, I moved to the United States for my PhD.

I'm into cosmology, especially the large-scale structure of the universe and the mysterious stuff we call dark energy. I'm part of the DESI collaboration, helping map the cosmos using light from millions of galaxies. It's a cool mix of theory, data, and trying to figure out how the universe works on the biggest scales.

Outside of research, I'm an avid stargazer, and I love writing and performing jokes about day-to-day happenings in the world (for the comedy savants reading this, the genre is "Topical Political Comedy"). I also like finding creative ways to share science, through writing, visualizations, or just chatting about the cosmos. Feel free to look around and see what I'm up to!

Research

Although I'm interested in all things cosmology, my PhD is mainly focused on understanding the nature of dark energy, large-scale structure, modified gravity, and full-shape analyses of the power spectrum and bispectrum. Below I have tried to classify my research into a few broader topics. Each tile takes you to a page where I have described the research in more detail, along with a quick (hopefully beginner friendly) background of the research topic.

Cosmological Tensions

Looking into the specific features in recent data which might be driving the Hubble Tension and the evidence for DDE. Click to know more!

Full Shape Analysis

Developed a pipeline to perform joint Power Spectrum and Bispectrum fits using the clustering data, combining EFT and phenomenological approaches to better model small scales and derive tighter parameter constraints. Click to know more!

Baryon Acoustic Oscillations

Performed validation checks including hexadecapole (P4) measurements in the fits for the DESI DR2 BAO key paper. Click to know more!

Modified Gravity

Contributing to the pipeline development for DESI DR2 Modified Gravity analysis. Click to know more!

CMB Compression

Worked on an optimal compression of the computation heavy CMB likelihood into a few parameters suitable for Dark Energy constraints. Click to know more!

21 cm Cosmology

Derived constraints on Coulomb-like DM interaction using the SARAS3 global 21cm data
